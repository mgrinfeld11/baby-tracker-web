How reminders work

Whenever you log a feed, the next reminder is automatically scheduled for last feed time + feed interval − reminder lead. So if you feed at 2:00pm with a 3-hour interval and 20-minute lead, the reminder fires at 4:40pm.

If she eats early, log it and the reminder shifts forward automatically — no manual reset needed.
